Summary: 100% Offline & Zero Data Collection

Vectralux does not collect, transmit, store on servers, track, or share any personal information, media, documents, or biometric credentials. The application is completely air-gapped and operates 100% on your device with no Internet connection requested or used.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and procedures of Vectralux (referred to as "the App", "we", "us", or "our") regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application on Android devices.

Vectralux is a dual-utility suite featuring hardware illumination tools (LED flashlight, strobe, SOS beacon, night reading lamp, bubble level, and offline document reader) alongside a private, PIN/Pattern-protected file encryption vault.

2. Zero Internet Permission (Air-Gapped by Design)

Vectralux is built with privacy as a foundational principle. The application’s manifest explicitly omits the android.permission.INTERNET permission.

  • The App cannot connect to the Internet, remote servers, cloud infrastructure, or external APIs.
  • We operate zero remote servers, zero databases, and zero cloud backups.
  • There are zero analytics SDKs, advertising libraries, or telemetry trackers embedded in the App.

4. Cryptographic Security & Vault Data Protection

Files, photos, videos, and notes imported into the Private Vault are secured using industry-standard cryptography:

  • AES-256-GCM Encryption: All vault items are encrypted with authenticated AES-256-GCM before being stored to disk.
  • PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 Key Derivation: Encryption keys are derived using strong PBKDF2 with cryptographically random 128-bit salts and unique 96-bit initialization vectors (IVs) generated per file.
  • App-Internal Sandbox: Encrypted blobs are camouflaged with .dat extensions and stored exclusively within the private internal app sandbox (context.filesDir), inaccessible to third-party file explorers on non-rooted devices.
  • Screen & Recents Protection (FLAG_SECURE): While inside the vault, the screen is flagged with FLAG_SECURE, preventing unauthorized screenshots, screen recording, and app switcher preview caching.
  • Backup Exclusion: Android automatic cloud backup and adb backup are strictly disabled (android:allowBackup="false") to prevent data extraction via USB or device syncing.

5. Decoy Vault & Duress Protection

Vectralux provides an optional decoy vault feature for coercion resistance. Entering the decoy PIN opens an isolated vault space with its own independent encryption key and storage directory. The decoy vault has zero knowledge of, or access to, your real master vault contents.

6. Data Retention & Secure Shredding

You maintain complete ownership and control over your data:

  • Export / Restore: You may decrypt and export any file back to your public device storage at any time.
  • Cryptographic Shredding: Deleting files or intruder logs from the vault overwrites the underlying disk sectors with random bytes before unlinking the file.
  • Uninstall Deletion: Uninstalling the application immediately deletes the app sandbox and all encrypted files stored within it by the operating system.
